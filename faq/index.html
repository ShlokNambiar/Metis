What Metis does

What does Metis actually build?

We are a product studio. We build and run AI systems that do a specific job inside a business — most often AI voice agents, WhatsApp funnels, and internal tools.

We are not a staffing firm and we are not a reseller for someone else's platform. Every engagement ends with software that runs in production and a person on our side responsible for it continuing to run.

Are you an AI consultancy or a development shop?

Closer to a studio than either. We take responsibility for the outcome, not just the build.

A consultancy hands you a recommendation. A development shop hands you a repository. We would rather own the thing that has to work on Monday morning, which means we tend to say no to projects we would not be able to operate.

What industries do you work with?

Most of our production work is in Indian insurance distribution. We also build internal software for technology companies and lead systems for real-estate tech.

The common thread is operational: businesses with a lot of repeated customer contact, where the work is real but the volume makes it expensive to do well by hand.

How big is the team?

Small and deliberately so. You will work directly with the people building your system.

There is no account layer between you and the engineering. For the size of engagement we take on, that is a feature — questions get answered by whoever wrote the code.

Working together

How do engagements usually start?

With a scoped paid pilot — a fixed fee, a fixed timeframe, and a number we agree in advance that tells us whether it worked.

We prefer this to an open-ended retainer because it puts the risk in the right place. If the pilot does not hit the number, you have spent a defined amount and learned something concrete rather than committing to a year.

What does it cost?

Pilots are quoted as a fixed fee after a scoping conversation. Production systems move to a monthly rate.

We do not publish a single number because the honest answer depends on call volume, how many languages you need, and how many of your existing systems we have to integrate with. Those change the figure by more than any list price would usefully convey. A scoping call takes about twenty minutes and produces a real number.

How long before something is live?

A voice pilot typically reaches live calls in two to three weeks, assuming telephony and data access are ready on your side.

The engineering is rarely the constraint. Number provisioning, DLT registration and getting clean customer data out of an existing system are what set the pace, and all three sit with your team or your telecom provider.

What do you need from us to start?

A contact list we are permitted to call, telephony access, and one person on your side who can make decisions.

The last one matters most. Pilots stall on unanswered questions far more often than on technical problems.

Do we own what you build?

Yes. Custom work we build for you is yours, set out in the engagement terms before we start.

Where a system depends on third-party services — telephony, model providers, messaging — those remain under their own terms, and we will tell you which ones you are taking on before you commit.

AI voice and calling

What can an AI voice agent realistically do?

Place or answer high volumes of routine calls, hold a real conversation in Hindi and English, work out whether the person is interested, and hand the ones who are to your team with context attached.

What it does not do is replace a closer. The value is in the front of the funnel — the calls your team does not have time to make, and the qualifying that has to happen before a human conversation is worth anyone's time.

Does it work in Hindi?

Yes, including customers who switch between Hindi and English mid-sentence, which is how most people actually speak.

Code-switching is the part that separates systems that work in India from systems that were built elsewhere and translated. It is worth testing specifically in any demo, ours included.

Will customers know they are talking to an AI?

Yes, and the agent says so. We do not build systems that pretend to be human.

In practice this costs less than people expect. Customers are largely fine with an automated call that is quick, useful and honest about what it is. They object to being deceived, not to being called by software.

Is AI cold calling legal in India?

Commercial calling is legal but regulated, and using an AI agent does not change any of the obligations.

Calls must originate from the designated number series — 1600 for service and transactional calls by regulated BFSI entities, 140 for promotional — rather than ordinary 10-digit numbers. The caller must be registered on the DLT platform. Promotional calls require DND scrubbing before every call and a verifiable consent record, and calling hours are restricted. We wrote the detail in our note on the 140 and 1600 series.

Can we hear a real call before deciding?

Yes. Ask, and we will arrange a live call rather than a recorded demo.

A demo reel proves a system worked once. We would rather you judged it on a call that is happening now — and we think you should ask this of every vendor you speak to.

Data and compliance

Where does our customer data go?

Into the systems required to run your agent, under a contract that covers processing on your behalf — and nowhere else.

We will tell you exactly which third parties are in the path before you commit, what each one receives, and how long it is kept. If that list changes, you hear about it before it changes.

Can Indian customer data be sent to an AI model hosted abroad?

Currently yes. India's DPDP framework permits transfer to any country the government has not specifically restricted, and no country has been restricted to date.

This is the opposite of the European position and it surprises most people. The obligations that do apply are consent, notice, retention and breach reporting. We set out the detail, including why you should still keep your model provider swappable, in our note on DPDP and AI.

What happens to call recordings and transcripts?

They are personal data, so they get a defined retention period tied to the purpose they were collected for, and they are deleted at the end of it.

Recordings are the artefact teams accumulate silently and never schedule for deletion. We set the schedule at the start of the engagement rather than after a breach makes it urgent.

Do you use our data to train models?

No. Your data is used to run your system.

We also check the terms of the providers in the path on your behalf, and confirm the same holds there.

Do you handle DLT registration and number provisioning?

We will guide it, but registration is tied to your entity, so parts of it can only be done by you.

We would rather be explicit about this early than have it surface as a surprise two weeks into a pilot. It is one of the more common reasons a launch date slips.

Practical

Where are you based?

India. We work with clients across the country and remotely.

Do you work with companies outside India?

Yes, though our deepest advantage is in the Indian market.

The regulatory and language knowledge that makes our Indian work good does not transfer to a US deployment. We will say so if a project would be better served by someone local to it.

What is the smallest project you take on?

A scoped pilot. Below that, an honest conversation is usually worth more to you than an engagement.

If what you need is advice rather than a build, we will give it in the first call and not invoice for it.
